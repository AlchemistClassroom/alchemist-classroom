Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Our tools run on your computer and don't send us any student data. We only get the details you give at checkout or when you email us.

Student data

Everything you enter in our tools — names, scores, notes — is saved on your computer or your school's network drive. We can't see it, collect it, or recover it. Keep your own backups.

When you buy

Payments are handled by our checkout provider. They collect your name, email, payment and billing details to process the order and send your download. We receive your name, email and order details so we can send updates and help with support. We never see your full card number.

This website

This site has no login and doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record your IP address.

Email

We use your email only for your order, product updates, and replies to your questions. We don't sell or share it. Ask us anytime to delete it.
